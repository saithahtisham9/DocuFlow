Privacy Policy

DocuFlow: Resize & Convert
Effective Date: September 11, 2026  •  Last Updated: September 11, 2026

1. Overview

DocuFlow: Resize & Convert ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. We believe that your personal photos, files, and documents belong solely to you.

DocuFlow is designed as a standalone, offline utility application. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict adherence to user privacy and data protection principles.

2. On-Device Processing & Zero Data Collection

DocuFlow operates exclusively on your device using native Apple iOS frameworks (including CoreGraphics, UIKit, PhotosUI, and PDFKit).

  • No External Uploads: Your images, photos, and generated PDF documents are processed entirely locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or backed up to our servers or any third-party cloud infrastructure.
  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location.
  • No Account Required: You can use all core tools and features of the App immediately without creating an account or logging in.

3. Photo Library & File Access

  • Explicit User Selection: DocuFlow utilizes Apple's modern PhotosPicker framework. The App only accesses the specific images or documents you intentionally select for editing, resizing, converting, or compressing.
  • No Background Library Scanning: The App does not index, scan, or monitor your broader photo library in the background.
  • Saving Outputs: When you choose to export or save a processed image or PDF document, the App uses the standard iOS Share Sheet to save the file directly to your device or your chosen local directory.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

  • Zero Third-Party SDKs: DocuFlow contains no third-party analytics SDKs, crash-reporting trackers, or behavioral profiling tools.
  • No Advertising: The App does not integrate any third-party ad networks, tracking identifiers (IDFA), or advertising cookies.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Because DocuFlow does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international regulations.

6. Security

Your data security is inherently guaranteed by Apple's iOS application sandboxing architecture. All file transformations execute inside the App's secure local environment and are discarded or saved only upon your direct action.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
